How it works(01)

Two modes, one workspace

QUICK NOTES

Revise fast

Built for the night before the exam. Paste or upload your material, pick a format, and get short, high-yield notes with only what's likely to be tested.

  • Nine revision formats, including MCQs and a graded quick test
  • Upload PDF, DOCX, TXT or Markdown
  • Export to PDF or Word in a click
KNOWLEDGE GRAPH

Understand deeply

For real mastery. Edgify reads your document, finds its concepts and the order they build in, and generates deep, source-grounded explanations on demand.

  • A prerequisite graph built from your own material
  • Learning readiness, study plans and a concept library
  • Per-concept explanations, quizzes and flashcards
